Which inspection do you need?

NFPA 211 decides this, not us. If any one of these is true, you need a Level 2.

1The house is being bought or sold
2A new stove, insert or fuel type is going in
3There has been a chimney fire, a strike or a bad storm

If none of them is true, the yearly sweep and Level 1 inspection at $140 is the one you want. Level 3 is for when access has to be opened up to find the problem. It is rare, and it is quoted.

Six places a chimney fails

What Chad is looking at while he is up there.

1

Cap

Missing or rusted through, and rain, leaves and nesting animals go straight down the flue.

2

Crown

Small cracks open up over a Kansas winter and let water into the brick from above.

3

Flue liner

Cracked tiles let heat and carbon monoxide reach the framing. Only a camera sees it.

4

Flashing

The usual cause of a leaking chimney that turns out to be a leaking roof.

5

Damper and smoke chamber

Where creosote collects first, and where a damper rusted open costs you all winter.

6

Firebox

Open mortar joints and burned-through panels. This is the finding that holds up house sales.

Creosote, in three degrees

One word for three different substances. Only the first comes off with a brush.

DegreeWhat it looks likeWhat it takes
FirstROUTINE Dusty, flaky soot. Comes off on a glove. A brush. This is what the yearly sweep is for.
SecondWORTH A TALK Hard black flakes, slightly shiny, packed into the corners. Rotary work, then a look at your draft and your firewood.
ThirdSTOP BURNING Glazed tar running down the tile like poured glass. Treatment over repeat visits, or a new liner. This is what catches fire.

Questions Chad gets on the phone

If yours is not here, call and ask. The answer is free either way.

How much does a chimney sweep cost?

$140 per flue for a sweep with a Level 1 inspection. $220 for a Level 2 camera inspection. No call-out fee and no separate estimate visit.

How often does a chimney need sweeping?

NFPA 211 calls for an inspection every year. Whether it needs sweeping depends on what the inspection finds: how much you burned, and how dry the wood was.

Do I need an inspection to sell my house?

Yes, a Level 2. A property sale is one of the three things NFPA 211 says calls for a camera inspection.

How long does it take, and how messy is it?

About an hour for one flue. Drop cloths go down, the opening is sealed, and a vacuum runs the whole time.

Can you install a wood stove or insert?

Yes. Chad is a certified installer, so it is his own work rather than something subcontracted out. Sizing, liner, clearances and hearth protection, then inspected before sign-off.
