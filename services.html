The visit

A realistic evaluation of your system.

Chad uses an NFPA-compliant, 33-point inspection format to document the condition of the chimney and hearth system.

  1. 01

    Take a first look

    Chad starts with the fireplace or stove and explains what comes next.

  2. 02

    Clean and inspect

    The firebox, smoke shelf, flue, chimney cap, and attached components are addressed as the service requires.

  3. 03

    Review the condition

    Areas of concern are noted so you can understand the condition of the property.

  4. 04

    Leave the area clean

    The work area is left clean because it is your home.
