What’s included

From the firebox to the chimney cap.

Chad cleans the firebox and chimney, including the smoke shelf, flue, chimney cap, and attached components that are part of the system.

Level 1 inspection included

The cleaning visit includes an NFPA-compliant Level 1 inspection and notes on any areas of concern.

The visit addresses

  • Firebox and smoke shelf
  • Flue and chimney interior
  • Chimney cap
  • Creosote buildup
  • Animal activity or blockages
  • Visible areas of concern

Why it matters

What builds up can change how the system performs.

Creosote buildup can contribute to a chimney fire. Animal activity can also block the chimney and affect performance.

A cleaning and inspection helps you understand the current condition before the next fire.

Common questions

Before you schedule

Is the price always $160?

Service starts at $160. A complex flue, severe creosote, multiple flues, or other conditions can add to the cost. Chad will discuss that with you before performing the service.

Is an inspection included?

Yes. Each chimney cleaning includes an NFPA-compliant Level 1 inspection.

What if I have more than one flue?

Multiple flues can affect the price and the time required. Call Chad with a description of the system before scheduling.

What happens if there is heavy buildup?

Severe creosote may require a different approach. Chad will explain what he finds and discuss the service before proceeding.
